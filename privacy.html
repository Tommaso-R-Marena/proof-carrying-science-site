Privacy

A static site by design.

This launch preview is intentionally built without an application backend, account system, advertising stack, or first-party analytics.

01

Pilot intake stays local

The intake builder constructs JSON in your browser. This static site does not send the form contents to Proof-Carrying Science.

02

No scientific uploads

Do not place confidential, patient, proprietary, or regulated scientific data into this website. Real pilots use a separately agreed data-handling process.

03

Hosting metadata

The hosting provider may process ordinary network-request metadata needed to serve the site. No PCS-controlled analytics or tracking scripts are currently configured.

Scope

The browser demo is explanatory only. It does not upload or verify real customer evidence, perform production Ed25519 verification, or replace the PCS executable checker. This notice should be reviewed before a public commercial launch or before adding analytics, forms, authentication, or server-side services.
