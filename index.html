How PCS works

The producer is not the trust boundary.

PCS separates evidence generation from evidence acceptance. Complex workflows may produce evidence; a smaller checker replays and binds that evidence to the scoped claim it is supposed to support.

01Declare

Claims, assumptions, workflow boundary, and reviewer expectations.

02Bind

Content-address data, code, model files, outputs, and runtime provenance.

03Check

Replay supported tests or independently check proof artifacts.

04Sign

Bind certificate, report, limitations, and artifacts into one authenticated package.

05Review

Apply an external acceptance policy chosen by the receiving organization.

Research program

From reproducibility to proof-carrying science.

The formal target is Γ ; E ⊢ C @ L: under explicit assumptions Γ, evidence E supports scoped claim C at assurance class L. Formal correctness, finite computation, empirical validation, and provenance remain distinct evidence classes.

What a PCS certificate does not mean.

A passing computational certificate does not establish that a biological model is adequate for a drug, that a therapy is safe or effective, that a workflow is regulator-approved or GxP validated, or that unsupported arbitrary code is correct. PCS is designed to make those boundaries explicit rather than blur them.
