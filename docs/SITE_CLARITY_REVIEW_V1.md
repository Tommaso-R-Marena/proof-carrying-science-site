# Page-by-page clarity review

All 75 authored website pages were reviewed. Forty-eight openings were rewritten;
already direct openings were retained. Every page has contextual instructions
and a short explanation of what its result or policy does and does not mean.
The optional glossary explains terms used on that page. It never runs a check,
changes a puzzle, captures input values, stores data or submits anything.

The scientific workflow is documented in [SCIENTIFIC_START_V1.md](SCIENTIFIC_START_V1.md).
Research, account, governance and contact pages have direct titles and first
steps. Privacy now begins with an overview before feature-specific policies.
Related research announcements follow the main explanation. Necessary technical
formats, proof statements, citations, legal detail and consent requirements are
retained. All original element IDs are preserved, including controlled runtime
fields; the verification algorithms and production authority are unchanged.

## Source integrity and historical evidence

The calibration, penguin and transport viewers had source-bound browser records.
Their old records and scientific artifacts remain unchanged. Exact previous HTML
snapshots and fresh browser reports are retained under `evidence/site-clarity-v1/`.
`verify_browser_ui_sources.mjs` requires all of the following:

- Original report bytes and previous HTML match their historical identities.
- Source-key coverage is unchanged, and all current sources match the fresh run.
- Every non-HTML mathematical, data and test-script source remains byte-identical.
- Every recorded numerical, negative-control and trust-boundary outcome remains
  exactly the same as the earlier qualification.
- The current reading-help files match their additional source identities.

The protected research-source check and quick copy tests invoke this verification.
Original public asset inventories, signed bundles, recorded receipts, formal
sources and earlier evidence are not rewritten to fit the new interface.
The calibration prose punctuation was simplified with the mathematical statement
preserved; the qualified-source guard remains enforced against the new tested UI.

## Limits of this review

Source review and automated browser checks establish functional behaviour,
readable rendering and preserved checks. They do not establish that actual
newcomers understand every page in a minute or enjoy the games. No human
participants were recruited or observed. The optional consent-based usability
kits remain available for that separate evaluation. Specialist mathematical
proofs and full policy detail remain technical where precision requires it.
