Task competency

Two ways to qualify for higher-trust work.

You do not have to wait for a preset calibration. Either take a newly generated competency evaluation or submit evidence/application for manual review. Auto-grading is screening only: PCS still makes the final approval manually.

PATH AVariable competency evaluation

PCS generates a fresh parameterized scenario set with shuffled choices for each attempt. Objective questions are auto-scored. A passing score goes to manual approval; it never grants authority automatically.

PATH BManual application / evidence review

Skip the evaluation and submit evidence explaining why you can responsibly perform and check this type of work. PCS targets a response within 1 business day and no later than 2 business days.

Path A · generated evaluation

Take a variable competency evaluation

Questions are generated from randomized parameters and shuffled answer order. There is no single fixed answer sheet. Up to three attempts per skill per 24 hours; manual application remains available at any time.

Path B · manual review

Submit competency evidence instead

Use prior work, coursework, research, code, formalization, or a concrete explanation of equivalent competence. The reviewer may ask follow-up questions. Target response: 1 business day; no later than 2 business days.

Account control

Delete my Commons account

This deletes your contributor account and its linked sessions, skill records, task requests, submissions, and account notifications. Enter your password to confirm. This does not delete unrelated public GitHub contributions or short-retention infrastructure logs.

NO TASK SQUATTING

Lower-level work is non-exclusive. Higher-level work is never reserved while waiting for approval.

Reserved L2+ work gets a progress checkpoint within 24 hours. Miss it and the reservation is automatically released so qualified people are not blocked for weeks.
