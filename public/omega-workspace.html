FIXED-SETTING REPLICATION
Three seeds. The same research limits.
Two additional CPU runs kept every model setting fixed. At eight checker calls, the bag head solved 73/96 on each seed, versus structural search's 68, 64 and 66. At four calls, all learned heads lost on every seed; the graph and bandit models also lost at eight calls. Across the three runs, Lean checked 179 generated targets without axioms and rejected all three false-original controls.
These are public generated families. Tasks may overlap across seeds, so this is not 288 independent external problems or evidence of general scientific intelligence. The workspace keeps the original checkpoint regardless of the replicate results.
