What did PCS actually prove here?

A countermodel is an explicitly constructed finite interpretation where the two displayed formulas disagree. An accepted repair only agrees with the selected formula over all enumerated finite worlds within the current 1–3-agent bound. It is not universal first-order equivalence, Lean elaboration, a kernel-checkable proof, proof of human English meaning, or real-world AI safety. Automatic repair search uses a limited grammar and may fail despite a possible repair.
