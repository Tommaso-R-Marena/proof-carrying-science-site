Funding the common good

Use scarce money where volunteerism is not enough.

PCS should make it easy to donate time, but it should not depend on highly skilled people being able to work for free. Non-dilutive funding and commercial revenue can support infrastructure, specialist review, and paid contributor work.

Funding sources

Different money can support the same public mission.

The legal and accounting treatment must remain explicit: grant, gift, sponsorship, commercial revenue, or contract should never be blurred together.

Funding firewall

Money must never buy a green result.

01

No purchased proof

Funding a project gives no sponsor the right to determine whether an obligation passes.

02

Conflict disclosure

Sponsored work, paid review, and organizational relationships should be visible on the relevant assurance case.

03

Negative results protected

A falsification or OPEN conclusion remains a valid deliverable even when the sponsor hoped for acceptance.
