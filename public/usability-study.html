How these results should be used
These are voluntary client records, not signed evidence of who performed a task. Browser clocks and answers can be changed. Automated sessions are labeled and must be excluded from human-study findings. Actual newcomer results require consenting participants and an observer’s separate study notes.
No human usability finding is asserted by publishing this page. Synthetic browser tests establish functionality; they do not establish that newcomers find PCS easy.
