ASSURANCE FOLD · FOLDIT-INSPIRED

Fold the evidence package until the honesty energy drops.

Drag tiles into the right assurance lanes—authority, computational support, scientific openness, rejection, and limitations. Like Foldit, you explore a configuration space and get an objective score. Unlike Foldit, a perfect fold still never certifies science.

Boundaries: This is an educational local game. It does not run Lean, does not issue ACCEPT/PASS, does not establish clinical validity, and does not recommend merges. Practice stays in your browser unless you separately use other PCS research opt-ins.
