Each challenge must define its own scientific objective, score direction, validation rule, and authority boundary. The first challenge is deliberately narrow so we can validate the scoring and review model before adding more.

What Arena is good for

Search spaces with fast, inspectable feedback.

Good Arena candidates include minimal counterexamples, adversarial test-case discovery, proof-search subproblems, canonicalization edge cases, mutation-based falsification, and other bounded problems where a candidate can be objectively checked or manually gated before ranking.

What Arena is bad for

Do not gamify judgment into a fake number.

Marketing quality, administrative reliability, literature synthesis, nuanced peer review, and broad scientific significance should not be compressed into a score simply to make them competitive.
