Ongoing contribution roles

Some useful work is a responsibility, not a ticket.

Recurring operations, communications, outreach, and community responsibilities belong here. Applying for a role never reserves a research task or automatically grants technical authority. PCS manually reviews fit, reliability, availability, and scope.

Nontechnical roles welcome:Operations QA, introductory documentation, outreach research, design review and newcomer support now have bounded recurring responsibilities. Roles are volunteer, manually approved, and never grant scientific authority. Apply for a technical skill review only if the chosen role requires one.

Why separate roles?

Recurring work should not distort the task graph.

A task should have a finish line and feed a concrete need. A role is permission and responsibility to perform a class of recurring work over time. PCS keeps the two separate so contribution metrics stay meaningful.

No authority shortcut

Operations access is not scientific authority.

Being approved for outreach, communications, or QA does not raise technical level, verify a research skill, or let someone approve assurance claims.
