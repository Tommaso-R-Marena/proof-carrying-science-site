How to read a result

Look for the actual execution mode, model or winning baseline, source revision, scope, assumptions, and downloadable evidence. The public proof service rechecks its approved source before dispatch. Its status is an observation, not a reservation or a proof.

Browser code delivery, the host, GitHub execution, the Lean toolchain and implementation correspondence remain trust dependencies. A successful formal check proves its encoded statement under its listed assumptions. It does not by itself establish English intent, empirical validity or general AI safety.
