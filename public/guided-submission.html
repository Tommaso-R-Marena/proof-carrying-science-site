What happens next

PCS separates five questions that are often blurred together.

1Is this the intended claim?You confirm the typed predicate and bound artifacts.
2Are these the same bytes?PCS re-hashes and binds the exact project/package contents.
3Did the required checks pass?The reviewer independently replays the declared evidence.
4What support follows?PCS derives the claim status from evidence; it does not trust a recorded PASS.
5Will this reviewer accept it?Acceptance is a separate receiver-owned policy decision.
How to read a PCS result

A green result never means “science proved true.”

VALID PACKAGE The delivered package passed structural, integrity, signature, and replay requirements.

It means the verifier accepted the exact package under PCS rules. It does not by itself establish the scientific claim.

COMPUTATIONALLY_SUPPORTED All declared computational evidence required by this claim passed.

It supports the exact typed predicate under its declared assumptions and scope. It does not automatically establish empirical, biological, or clinical truth.

FALSIFIED_OR_CHECK_FAILED At least one required check failed.

The claim cannot receive the requested support from this evidence. Investigate the failed check rather than treating the package as successful.

OPEN Required evidence is missing, unverified, or insufficient for that claim type.

OPEN is not a pass and not necessarily a falsification. It means PCS cannot currently justify the requested assurance level.

ACCEPTED The reviewer’s explicit policy accepts the verified package and claim statuses.

Acceptance is governance, not scientific truth. Two reviewers may apply different policies to the same valid package.
