Safety Forge: dynamic game and opt-in adult research

Game play is local by default. Safety Forge uses a deterministic fictional agent simulator in your browser. You can explore a generated seed, experiment with actions and candidate shields, replay failures, and run a bounded safety check without signing in or uploading those choices. Normal page requests and Cloudflare aggregate analytics still apply.

Optional research donation is only for self-declared adults aged 18 or older with a signed-in PCS account and verified email. You must explicitly turn on the adult-consent switch for that mission and click Donate research replay to submit it; switching on alone does not upload data. The server independently reruns every submitted candidate and records the versioned scenario seed, action sequences, chosen safety guard combinations, whether hints or earlier checker feedback were disclosed before the choice, simulated transitions, risk/invariant violations, bounded checker decisions, and submission time. We link the session internally to your account to prevent abuse and allow erasure. We do not independently verify your age, expertise, or actual independent discovery.

Only the PCS Founder/Owner can export Safety Forge samples for research. Exports exclude account ID, display name, email, IP address, and exact submission time. The results may be used in internal experiments with synthetic policy-repair and counterexample-search models, but are not real Lean 4 proof traces or verified claims about deployed AI systems. You can delete donated sessions from the Safety Forge page or delete your account, but earlier research exports, backups, or model weights may not be fully retractable after use. We do not accept minors' research data. Never put confidential research or identifying information into game records.

Proof Quest play and optional adult-only research data

Proof Quest can be played by anyone without signing in. The game does not send practice choices or scores to PCS. The website may still receive ordinary page requests and aggregate Cloudflare Web Analytics as described below.

Optional research donation is for adults 18+ only. To contribute, you must deliberately turn on a clearly labeled switch confirming you are 18+ and consent to donating the attempt, sign into an account with a verified email address, and click Share finished run to submit the completed ordering. Self-declared age is not independently identity-verified. Children's game attempts are not eligible for research collection. We store the puzzle ID and its checker version (including the seed embedded in a procedurally generated puzzle ID), ordered step IDs, hints used, deterministic score, validity label, timestamp, and account ID to check research examples, enforce limits, and support deletion. The server independently reconstructs procedural puzzles from their seed and does not trust the browser's score. Coach, X-ray and guided correction usage is included in the existing hint count. The dataset is used internally to evaluate possible proof-order planning heuristics and machine-learning methods; it is not equivalent to actual Lean 4 proof trajectories.

The account holder can use Proof Quest → Delete all my donated attempts to remove active-database rows, and deleting the account also removes linked research entries. Only the Founder/Owner can export a dataset without names, emails, IP addresses, or account IDs. Existing exported copies and short-lived infrastructure backups may have separate retention, so removal from all historic copies cannot be guaranteed by the button. Avoid submitting any confidential or personal information.

Contributor submission privacy and GitHub staging

When you deliberately submit work through the Commons, PCS stores your summary, verification notes, AI disclosure, outreach-evidence classification, and any attached text files in its account database. For tasks explicitly marked as GitHub-enabled, the uploaded files and a digest manifest may be copied to a separate contribution branch and pull request in a PCS GitHub repository. GitHub access, retention, and visibility then also apply. Never submit passwords, access tokens, private research data, unpublished results you cannot share, or private details about people you contacted.

A passing automated check does not certify scientific truth; administrators review submissions before accepting, and only the Founder/Owner can invoke integration through the PCS dashboard. Evidence of outreach activity is not treated as proof of clicks, registrations, or business results.

Privacy

Local by design.

PCS now has a small account backend for the AI Safety Commons. Contributor accounts, verified levels/skills, task applications, review decisions, notifications, and audit events are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database in the United States. Scientific package inspection, Project Mapper, browser verifier, and pilot-intake tools remain local in the browser and do not upload scientific file contents to PCS. The site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic/Core Web Vitals and Workers Logs/Issues for operational reliability; request query strings are redacted before observability persistence.

01

Project Mapper stays in-browser

Files you explicitly select are read and SHA-256 hashed locally to draft artifact/check metadata. Python/Jupyter text may also be inspected locally for literal file dependencies; it is never executed. The mapper contains no PCS upload endpoint or network API.

02

Verifier stays in-browser

The legacy browser verifier uses Web Crypto and JavaScript locally. Compatible package JSON and selected extracted-directory files are not transmitted by this site.

03

Intake stays in-browser

The pilot-intake builder validates, hashes, freezes, and downloads your intake locally. The page does not submit form contents to PCS.

04

Privacy-first site analytics

Cloudflare serves the site and provides Web Analytics/Core Web Vitals measurement. Workers Logs and Issues are enabled only for operational reliability and error diagnosis; request query strings are redacted before observability persistence. PCS does not use advertising pixels, behavioral ad targeting, or a scientific-data upload endpoint. No scientific file contents are sent to PCS; Package Inspector, Project Mapper, verifier, and intake contents remain local unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Do not upload sensitive science here

This website is not the customer data plane.

Do not place confidential scientific artifacts, direct identifiers, PHI, signing keys, regulated records, or proprietary datasets into this website. A real design-partner pilot uses a separately agreed data-handling process appropriate to the workflow.

AI Safety Commons account state

Account authority is server-side; scientific project files are still local.

ACCOUNT

Cloudflare D1

The Commons stores your name, email, password-derived verifier, recovery-code hash, verified PCS level, profile preferences, skill-review state, task applications, submissions, decisions, notifications, and audit records. Passwords and recovery codes are not stored in plaintext.

SESSIONS

Secure session cookie

Sign-in uses an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite cookie. Sessions expire after 30 days. Account recovery uses the one-time recovery code shown at signup; PCS stores only its cryptographic hash.

TASKS

Applications + review

Higher-level applications, AI-use plans, verification plans, checkpoints, and review outcomes are stored so PCS can enforce levels, verified skills, deadlines, reservation release, and contributor rights. L0 open work remains non-exclusive.

Browser localStorageThe earlier local-only Commons planning profile has been replaced by the account backend. Other local scientific tools may still use ordinary browser state for UI convenience, but selected scientific file contents are not sent to the Commons account database.
Email + account deletion

Notifications are durable even if email delivery is unavailable.

Task approvals and review results are stored in your PCS account. Transactional email is sent only when PCS has an explicitly configured email provider. You can delete your account from the account interface after confirming your password; related sessions, skills, requests, submissions, and user notifications are deleted through database relationships, while minimal security/operational logs may remain for their normal retention period.
