Privacy

Local by design.

The current PCS website is static. It has no account system, application backend, database, advertising stack, first-party analytics, or scientific-data upload endpoint.

01

Verifier stays in-browser

The verifier uses Web Crypto and JavaScript in your browser. Compatible package JSON and files selected through the extracted-directory picker are read locally and are not transmitted by this site.

02

Intake stays in-browser

The pilot-intake builder validates, hashes, freezes, and downloads your intake locally. The page does not submit form contents to PCS.

03

Hosting metadata

Cloudflare may process ordinary network-request metadata necessary to serve the site. PCS has not added a first-party analytics or tracking SDK.

Do not upload sensitive science here

This website is not the customer data plane.

Do not place confidential scientific artifacts, direct identifiers, PHI, signing keys, regulated records, or proprietary datasets into this website. A real design-partner pilot uses a separately agreed data-handling process appropriate to the workflow.

Questions about a pilot or data boundary?

Contact Tommaso directly before sending sensitive materials.
