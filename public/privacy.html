Meaning Forge: optional translation research and independent annotation review

Gameplay is private by default. English-to-Lean and Lean-to-English exercises run in your browser using a bounded, publicly disclosed library of synthetic logical propositions. Starting a game, editing a formula, receiving hints or checking a structural answer does not send a research trace to PCS. Ordinary HTTP requests and aggregate Cloudflare security/analytics telemetry still apply.

Research donation requires a signed-in PCS account, verified email, an explicit self-confirmation of age 18 or older, an affirmative research-consent checkbox, and a separate Donate action. The backend independently replays the selected mission, candidate edits, hints and final proposition. It saves an account-linked replay record, the typed predicates, a score and the written explanation you voluntarily provided. The explanation is not automatically treated as correct training text.

Qualified L5/L6 reviewers with independently verified review skills (or the Founder/Owner) can inspect human explanations and record an immutable label. Two independent faithful reviews with no conflicting label are required before the text can be included as eligible annotation in owner-only exports. Reviewers cannot label their own sessions. Research exports omit email, account identity, IP address and precise timestamps, and suppress unreviewed text.

The game contains no general English understanding model and does not execute Lean's kernel. All gameplay feedback concerns a narrow explicit proposition grammar, not arbitrary scientific truth. Participants may erase their active donation records using the Erase control while signed in; dependent reviewer labels are removed as well. Already-created offline exports or downstream model copies may require independent action and may not be fully retractable.

Independent senior-review recommendations on task submissions are recorded for governance; the actual production code merge remains under Owner control after exact-head CI and human review.

ProofLab: real-source theorem investigations and optional adult research

Playing is local by default. ProofLab presents 22 existing source-indexed Lean theorem targets as controlled next-investigation puzzles. Each decision shows a deterministic set of 2–4 plausible candidates. Your choices, blockers, stated reasons, confidence and suggested falsification remain in the page unless you explicitly donate; personal XP and completed mission IDs can be stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing local XP removes that browser copy. Ordinary website requests and aggregate analytics still apply.

ProofLab does not execute Lean or generate new verified proofs. The Worker independently checks only the known educational graph prerequisites and refuses unknown or withheld cases. Eight validation and ten evaluation theorem targets remain privately reserved; public players do not receive those challenge graphs.

Research donation requires a signed-in account with verified email, two separate affirmative checkboxes (self-attested age 18+ and informed consent), and a separate Donate button. Submitted fields are the fixed case ID, version, bounded planning actions, reason tags, confidence values, explicit hint-exposure flags, and an unverified choice from four predefined adversarial topics. The server reconstructs the exact candidate set and feasible subset that should have existed before every move; the browser cannot submit its own labels. We do not solicit raw free text, mouse trajectories, reaction-time fingerprints or minors' gameplay. The account link is retained privately solely for rate limits, deduplication and deletion.

Owner-only export strips user identity and precise timestamps; participants can delete active linked rows through the ProofLab page. Offline exports and already trained models may not be completely retractable. An account or browser cannot prove that a submitted choice was genuinely human or made before revealing hints.

Shield Duel: blind judgments and optional adult research

Playing is local by default. In this synthetic eight-round safety comparison game, your preferences, reason choices and confidence stay in your browser session. Normal page requests still reach the website.

Only self-attested adults 18+ who explicitly check two research boxes, have a signed-in verified-email PCS account and separately click Donate may share research data. One explicit click submits four small, two-ballot batches for the free-tier checker: exact round/seed, which policy you selected, reason code, confidence, and a version identifier. No free text, mouse movement, raw timing, children's game data, or automatic gameplay uploads are collected.

The server regenerates both guard policies and their bounded finite-state labels independently. It cannot verify that a submitted vote was genuinely human or recorded before its answer was displayed. Data is account-linked privately to enable deletion and daily limits; Owner-only exports omit personal identifiers, IPs and exact times. Offline copies or trained weights might not be fully retractable after export. You can delete active donated sessions from within the game.

Safety Forge: dynamic game and opt-in adult research

Game play is local by default. Safety Forge uses a deterministic fictional agent simulator in your browser. You can explore a generated seed, experiment with actions and candidate shields, replay failures, and run a bounded safety check without signing in or uploading those choices. Normal page requests and Cloudflare aggregate analytics still apply.

Optional research donation is only for self-declared adults aged 18 or older with a signed-in PCS account and verified email. You must explicitly turn on the adult-consent switch for that mission and click Donate research replay to submit it; switching on alone does not upload data. The server independently reruns every submitted candidate and records the versioned scenario seed, action sequences, chosen safety guard combinations, whether hints or earlier checker feedback were disclosed before the choice, simulated transitions, risk/invariant violations, bounded checker decisions, and submission time. From that replay it derives search-and-repair labels such as blind counterexample discovery, distance from a shortest bounded failure witness, sequential guard additions/removals, score and cost deltas, Pareto improvements, and the best verified repair. We link the session internally to your account to prevent abuse and allow erasure. We do not independently verify your age, expertise, or actual independent discovery.

Only the PCS Founder/Owner can export Safety Forge samples for research. Exports exclude account ID, display name, email, IP address, and exact submission time. The results may be used in internal experiments with synthetic policy-repair and counterexample-search models, but are not real Lean 4 proof traces or verified claims about deployed AI systems. You can delete donated sessions from the Safety Forge page or delete your account, but earlier research exports, backups, or model weights may not be fully retractable after use. We do not accept minors' research data. Never put confidential research or identifying information into game records.

Proof Quest play and optional adult-only research data

Proof Quest can be played by anyone without signing in. The game does not send practice choices or scores to PCS. The website may still receive ordinary page requests and aggregate Cloudflare Web Analytics as described below.

Optional research donation is for adults 18+ only. To contribute, you must deliberately turn on a clearly labeled switch confirming you are 18+ and consent to donating the attempt, sign into an account with a verified email address, and click Share finished run to submit the completed ordering. Self-declared age is not independently identity-verified. Children's game attempts are not eligible for research collection. We store the puzzle ID and its checker version (including the seed embedded in a procedurally generated puzzle ID), ordered step IDs, hints used, deterministic score, validity label, timestamp, and account ID to check research examples, enforce limits, and support deletion. The server independently reconstructs procedural puzzles from their seed and does not trust the browser's score. Coach, X-ray and guided correction usage is included in the existing hint count. The dataset is used internally to evaluate possible proof-order planning heuristics and machine-learning methods; it is not equivalent to actual Lean 4 proof trajectories.

The account holder can use Proof Quest → Delete all my donated attempts to remove active-database rows, and deleting the account also removes linked research entries. Only the Founder/Owner can export a dataset without names, emails, IP addresses, or account IDs. Existing exported copies and short-lived infrastructure backups may have separate retention, so removal from all historic copies cannot be guaranteed by the button. Avoid submitting any confidential or personal information.

Contributor submission privacy and GitHub staging

When you deliberately submit work through the Commons, PCS stores your summary, verification notes, AI disclosure, outreach-evidence classification, and any attached text files in its account database. For an approved task that actually requires code, PCS exposes only curated GitHub file excerpts and line ranges needed for that task; a request for adjacent code is stored, audited, and requires Founder/Owner approval before that exact range is shown. The task interface does not provide arbitrary private-repository browsing. For tasks explicitly marked as GitHub-enabled, submitted work and a digest manifest may later be copied to a separate contribution branch and pull request in a PCS GitHub repository. GitHub access, retention, and visibility then also apply. Never submit passwords, access tokens, private research data, unpublished results you cannot share, or private details about people you contacted.

A passing automated check does not certify scientific truth; administrators review submissions before accepting, and only the Founder/Owner can invoke integration through the PCS dashboard. Evidence of outreach activity is not treated as proof of clicks, registrations, or business results.

Privacy

Local by design.

PCS now has a small account backend for the AI Safety Commons. Contributor accounts, verified levels/skills, task applications, review decisions, notifications, and audit events are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database in the United States. Scientific package inspection, Project Mapper, browser verifier, and pilot-intake tools remain local in the browser and do not upload scientific file contents to PCS. The site also uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregate traffic/Core Web Vitals and Workers Logs/Issues for operational reliability; request query strings are redacted before observability persistence.

01

Project Mapper stays in-browser

Files you explicitly select are read and SHA-256 hashed locally to draft artifact/check metadata. Python/Jupyter text may also be inspected locally for literal file dependencies; it is never executed. The mapper contains no PCS upload endpoint or network API.

02

Verifier stays in-browser

The legacy browser verifier uses Web Crypto and JavaScript locally. Compatible package JSON and selected extracted-directory files are not transmitted by this site.

03

Intake stays in-browser

The pilot-intake builder validates, hashes, freezes, and downloads your intake locally. The page does not submit form contents to PCS.

04

Privacy-first site analytics

Cloudflare serves the site and provides Web Analytics/Core Web Vitals measurement. Workers Logs and Issues are enabled only for operational reliability and error diagnosis; request query strings are redacted before observability persistence. PCS does not use advertising pixels, behavioral ad targeting, or a scientific-data upload endpoint. No scientific file contents are sent to PCS; Package Inspector, Project Mapper, verifier, and intake contents remain local unless a page explicitly says otherwise.

Do not upload sensitive science here

This website is not the customer data plane.

Do not place confidential scientific artifacts, direct identifiers, PHI, signing keys, regulated records, or proprietary datasets into this website. A real design-partner pilot uses a separately agreed data-handling process appropriate to the workflow.
