      What is shared, and how do I delete it?

      Playing is always free and private. The puzzle does not send your choices to PCS. Adults with a PCS account and verified email may separately choose to donate one completed ordering for research on prerequisite-order planning.

      The donated record contains the puzzle ID/version, ordered step IDs, hints used, quality score, and timestamp. It is connected privately to your account for withdrawal/deletion, but research exports omit your account ID. This is synthetic ordering data, not valid Lean traces or a promise that a model learned to prove theorems. No data from minors is accepted.

      About Proof Quest and its research
      🧩 PROOF QUEST · BEGINNER FRIENDLY

      Build a proof, one little step at a time.

      Eight friendly puzzles, an ever-changing set of generated scientific mysteries, an interactive dependency X-ray, and a detective notebook. Experiment freely; there can be several correct orders, and no coding is required.

      Important: This is not a Lean 4 proof and does not run Lean. It teaches the prerequisite-order idea using synthetic puzzles.
