For the person running a usability test
Invite participants separately, with consent. Do not coach during the first attempt. Ask them to explain what PCS does after about a minute. Observe completion, errors and requests for help. Use a separate observer record to document prior experience, help given and the actual session.
Browser records cannot establish human authorship, reliable elapsed time or task completion on their own. Automated records must be labeled and excluded from human findings. Ratings are self-reports. No newcomer usability or enjoyment result is claimed by publishing this kit.
