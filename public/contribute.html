Progression

Reliability earns access. Credentials are useful, but not the only path in.

Level controls task complexity and review authority. Compensation is attached to tasks rather than being a secret reward for reaching a rank.

Why L2 for paid work?

Paid-task eligibility should begin before review authority.

A reliable contributor should not have to reach senior-review status before compensation becomes possible. L2 is where the general paid marketplace opens after verified work. L4 is different: it is the point where PCS may authorize someone to review other contributors' evidence.

Qualification is not unpaid labor at scale.A small orientation and qualification task may be required. Productive work should be clearly labeled volunteer, funded bounty, paid review, or specialist contract before it begins.
Negative results matter

Finding that a claim fails can be the highest-value contribution.

PCS should never create pressure to make an assurance case turn green. Falsifications, unresolved assumptions, reviewer disagreements, and correctly OPEN obligations are valuable outputs.

  • Found a bypass? That counts.
  • Could not reproduce the result? That counts.
  • Found a missing assumption? That counts.
  • Showed a task is underspecified? That counts.
