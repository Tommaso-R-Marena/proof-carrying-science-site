What Meaning Forge verifies—and what it does not

Verified by replay: that submitted edits lead to the reported bounded expression, its canonical Lean template round-trips to the same structured claim, and its structure agrees or disagrees with a PCS-curated target.

Not verified: whether a human-written explanation is faithful, whether a particular empirical claim is true, or whether arbitrary Lean text compiles. No Aristotle outputs or AI-generated teacher labels are collected by this game.
