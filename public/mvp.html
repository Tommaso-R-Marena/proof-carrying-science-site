The complete loop

From a bounded workflow to a portable assurance package.

The producer never gets to declare itself accepted. PCS separates scientific replay, package validity, signer authenticity, and the receiving organization's acceptance policy.

ProducerBuild and attest
pcs attest-v06 project/manifest.json -o study.pcs.zip --private-key organization-private.pem --public-key organization-public.pem

Copies exact artifacts into the package first, replays supported checks against those copied bytes, builds the typed v0.6 certificate and normalized decision set, signs the package, verifies the candidate ZIP, then atomically publishes it.

ReviewerVerify + apply policy
pcs verify-v06-bundle study.pcs.zip --public-key trusted-public.pem --expected-signer-fingerprint <fingerprint> --policy reviewer-policy.json --receipt receipt.json

Reads the ZIP without extracting it, verifies the signer and exact file set, reruns supported scientific checks, regenerates normalized decisions, then applies the reviewer's external acceptance criteria.

Why policy is separate

Valid does not mean accepted.

A PCS package can be perfectly authentic and replay-consistent while honestly carrying a failed or open scientific claim. The receiving organization decides whether those claim statuses are acceptable for its use case.

PCS validityDid the delivered bytes, signatures, replay results, and normalized decisions agree?
Reviewer acceptanceDo the verified claim statuses and signer identity satisfy this reviewer's external policy?

Example policy

The producer does not embed or sign the reviewer's acceptance criteria. The reviewer supplies them at verification time.

{
  "policy_version": "pcs-acceptance-policy-v1",
  "require_signature": true,
  "required_claims": {
    "C_PKPD_CONTRACT": ["COMPUTATIONALLY_SUPPORTED"],
    "C_PKPD_REPLAY": ["COMPUTATIONALLY_SUPPORTED"]

}

Initial MVP scope

Five executable scientific checks, one strict delivery path.

01

PK/PD contract

Restricted one-compartment IV-bolus + direct Emax representation, units, dimensions, and positivity.

02

PK/PD replay

Independent analytic recomputation from packaged model bytes and exported predictions.

03

CSV disjointness

Verify declared split keys do not overlap between packaged CSV artifacts.

04

Reaction balance

Replay a narrow atom-balance predicate over declared stoichiometric terms.

05

Unit compatibility

Check supported dimensional expressions and conversion compatibility.

Boundary

External evidence stays explicit

Formal, empirical, statistical, and provenance evidence is not auto-promoted to PASS by the MVP producer.

Formal boundary

The executable MVP and the formal theorem stack are related, but not identical.

The current v0.6/v2 executable format has the full producer/reviewer product path. The strongest machine-checked raw-wire-to-Assures theorem stack currently targets the older v0.5/v1 wire, with package authenticity and replay represented through explicit theorem assumptions. Porting that theorem stack onto the exact v0.6/v2 representation remains open work.
