Distributed assurance

Contributors propose work. Verification controls authority.

A volunteer, specialist, or AI system can propose a decomposition, evidence item, test, proof, or attack. The proposal does not become authoritative because of who submitted it.

1
Root claim is typedDefine the exact bounded proposition and the committed computational world it refers to.
2
Dependencies become obligationsDecompose into formal, computational, empirical, reviewed, and OPEN nodes.
3
Human-addressable leaves become tasksEach task gets scope, time, acceptance criteria, funding status, and provenance.
4
Submissions are checkedMachine verification, independent reproduction, formal proof, or qualified review determines whether a leaf closes.
5
Root status is recomputedOnly accepted leaves and semantics-preserving composition can support the root. Missing or failed leaves remain visible.
