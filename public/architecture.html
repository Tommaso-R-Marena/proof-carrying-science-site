Technical architecture

A small acceptance path around a large scientific workflow.

PCS is designed around a simple trust principle: the system that produces a scientific result does not get to define whether its own evidence is sufficient. Producers emit artifacts and claims; a separate verification path replays supported checks, binds evidence, authenticates delivery, and applies reviewer-controlled policy.

1
Scientific producerAny simulator, notebook, model, AI system, optimization pipeline, or human-authored computation produces candidate artifacts.
2
PCS package constructionClaims, assumptions, artifacts, replayable check specifications, workflow structure, runtime provenance, limitations, and cryptographic hashes are bound into a deterministic evidence package.
3
Independent executable replayThe checker reruns supported predicates from delivered artifacts rather than trusting recorded PASS fields.
4
Decision semanticsEvidence outcomes are mapped to formal, computational, empirical, mixed, OPEN, or failed claim status under explicit rules.
5
Reviewer policyThe receiver decides which claim statuses and signer identity are sufficient for acceptance.

Trust statement

Formal verification does not certify biology.

The current PCS decision and normalized-state soundness theorems are machine-checked under Lean 4.28.0. They show that an accepted normalized decision yields the corresponding scoped assurance judgment under explicit context and evidence-binding assumptions. It does not prove that the underlying scientific model is empirically adequate for the world. PCS therefore keeps implementation correctness, computational replay, empirical validation, provenance, and reviewer policy as separate dimensions.

Correctness

Did the computation follow its declared rules?

Formal proof and executable replay can address this dimension within a bounded model.

Adequacy

Is the model scientifically appropriate?

This requires empirical/statistical evidence and domain judgment; formal proof alone cannot supply it.

Acceptance

Is the evidence enough for this reviewer?

That is an external policy decision, not something the producer should silently choose.
