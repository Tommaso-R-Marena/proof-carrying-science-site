Do not collapse “proved,” “checked,” and “trusted” into one green badge.

Filter the current PCS assurance stack by epistemic status. Each card states what the layer contributes and what remains outside that layer.

PROVED

Canonical v0.6/v2 representation

Machine-checked parsing/representation and package structure theorems connect accepted canonical values to the formal semantic layer.

Lean kernel remains foundational TCB.
PROVED

Package/member/hash/index binding

Formal theorems bind certificate, manifest, normalized index, exact member identities, sizes, and hash commitments.

Collision resistance is represented explicitly rather than silently assumed.
PROVED

Normalized decisions → scoped assurance

The formal decision stack proves what assurance follows for the exact typed claim under its declared evidence and assumptions.

This is not arbitrary scientific truth.
PROVED

reaction_balance replay faithfulness

The chemistry tokenizer/checker bridge is formally connected to its declared atom-balance semantics.

Other current built-ins have different assurance boundaries.
CHECKED

Exact ZIP/member SHA-256 binding

Production verification recomputes exact delivered bytes, package members, sizes, hashes, and signed control records before replay.

ZIP decoding/materialization remains an implementation boundary pending stricter raw-byte formalization.
CHECKED

Environment + workflow regeneration

Human-confirmed environment and static-workflow claims are regenerated from delivered source bytes rather than trusted as recorded PASS values.

Semantic fidelity remains explicit for unsupported/general cases.
CHECKED

Scientific replay

Supported evidence items are recomputed and normalized decisions must equal the fresh replay-derived decision set.

Replay fidelity outside formally closed checkers remains an external contract.
CONTRACT

Archive decoder / filesystem / invocation

Operational decoding, materialization, process invocation, and host runtime behavior are named TCB rather than inheriting formal-verification language.

Target: shrink with strict canonical ZIP/raw-byte authority.
CONTRACT

Independent crypto correspondence

PCS has Lean SHA-256/Ed25519 specifications and wiring proofs; independent correspondence and no-forgery assumptions remain explicit boundaries.

No claim that pure formalization proves real-world cryptographic unforgeability.
CONTRACT

Capture/reconstruction/replay fidelity

Environment capture and non-chemistry replay require contracts connecting executable observation to the intended external semantics.

These are targets for checker-specific closure.
NOT CLAIMED

Biological or clinical truth

A valid PCS package supports its exact typed computational claim. It does not automatically establish biological efficacy, clinical validity, or causal truth.

NOT CLAIMED

Regulatory approval / GxP validation

PCS assurance is not itself regulatory authorization, quality-system certification, or a substitute for domain governance.

NOT CLAIMED

Correctness of arbitrary code

PCS is deliberately fail-closed and scoped to supported predicates/checkers. Unsupported software does not become proved merely by entering a package.
