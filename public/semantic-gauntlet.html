How does PCS verify this?

From game to measurable research

PCS provides a companion reproducible benchmark CLI. Models must answer every case, produce genuine finite countermodels or explicitly abstain, and are evaluated by family and skill. Open-source evaluation cases are not blind; results must not be portrayed as unseen scientific generalization.
