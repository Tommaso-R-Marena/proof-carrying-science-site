30-second reading guide

PCS is answering four different questions.

1ClaimWhat exact computational statement is being asserted?
2EvidenceDo the required checks independently replay on the delivered bytes?
3SupportWhat assurance status follows from that evidence?
4PolicyDoes this reviewer accept that verified support level?

Important: an accepted package does not mean “the science is universally true.” It means the exact package passed this verifier and satisfies this reviewer’s explicit policy.
