Live browser verifier

Verify the evidence, then break it.

This is a functional, client-side assurance demo. Your browser verifies a real Ed25519 signature and SHA-256 digest, independently replays a synthetic one-compartment PK + direct Emax PD model, derives claim status, and applies reviewer policy. Toggle tampering to see the checks fail for different reasons.

Evidence package

The built-in reference is synthetic and signed with a dedicated demo-only Ed25519 key.
