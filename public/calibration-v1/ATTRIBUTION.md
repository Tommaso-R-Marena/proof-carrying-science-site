# External experimental data

Gerritsma, J., Onnink, R., & Versluis, A. (1981). Yacht Hydrodynamics [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5XG7R.

Original measurement bytes are retained unchanged from the UCI archive. Dataset: CC BY 4.0, separate from first-party PCS code. License and attribution must accompany redistribution. Derived analysis, row selections and polynomial transformations are PCS-authored and explicitly described in the protocol. UCI describes 308 experiments across 22 hull forms at the Delft Ship Hydromechanics Laboratory. No fresh physical experiment or NIST endorsement is claimed.
