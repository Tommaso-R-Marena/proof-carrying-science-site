    What this experiment does—and does not—establish

    The repaired formula agrees with its selected structured source in all finite models within the challenge’s declared domain bound. That does not prove unrestricted first-order equivalence, match the intent of arbitrary human English, or certify any deployed AI system. These tasks are public and synthetic, so results are neither blind evaluation nor real-world generalization.

    Critical baseline: the structured source itself is available. Simply copying that AST can pass this toy checker in one new check. We measure whether a model can navigate edits, not whether it solved a difficult natural-language formalization problem.
