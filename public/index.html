The product

Evidence that travels with the claim.

Scientific results often arrive as a collection of code, data, notebooks, exported figures, model files, and informal explanations. PCS turns a bounded computational claim into a structured assurance package another person can inspect and replay without trusting the original producer.

01

Map the project

Start from existing files. PCS drafts candidate artifacts, checks, claims, and assumptions for explicit human review.

02

Bind the evidence

Content-address model files, outputs, code, provenance, runtime state, and machine-readable checks.

03

Verify the delivery

Replay supported checks, authenticate the package, apply reviewer policy, and preserve unresolved limitations.

How it works

The generator is not the security boundary.

A complex workflow can produce evidence. PCS is designed so the acceptance decision comes from a smaller, independently inspectable verification path.

Research architecture

Verification is not validation.

PCS keeps formal correctness, computational replay, empirical validation, provenance, and reviewer policy separate. A correct implementation of a model does not by itself show that the model is biologically adequate.

Γ

Explicit assumptions

Scientific claims are interpreted under a named context rather than hidden background assumptions.

E

Typed evidence

Formal proof, computational checks, empirical validation, and provenance are different evidence classes.

C @ L

Scoped assurance

The formal target is Γ ; E ⊢ C @ L: evidence supports a scoped claim at an explicit assurance level.

First commercial wedge

Computational biopharma, one bounded workflow at a time.

The first PCS adapter focuses on a restricted one-compartment IV-bolus PK model with a direct Emax PD layer. The goal is not to certify pharmacology in general. It is to prove that a bounded computational claim can be reviewed with substantially stronger evidence than a conventional handoff.

A

Model contract

Units, dimensions, positivity, and declared representation constraints.

B

Analytic replay

Independent recomputation of declared PK/PD equations and exported predictions.

C

Provenance

Runtime, artifacts, hashes, package identity, and signed limitations.

D

Reviewer control

The receiving organization—not the producer—defines acceptable claim status.
