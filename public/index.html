The product in one picture

Separate the scientific claim from the machinery around it.

Most computational handoffs collapse code, data, results, signatures, and organizational approval into one opaque “trust me.” PCS keeps each layer explicit and independently inspectable.

Trust without hand-waving

What is proved, what is checked, and what is still trusted are different things.

The current v0.6/v2 theorem stack connects accepted canonical package structure and normalized decisions to scoped scientific assurance. Production validity is gated on compiled Lean acceptance. Remaining external contracts stay visible rather than inheriting a “formally verified” label.
