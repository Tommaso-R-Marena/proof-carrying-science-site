Distributed assurance

From “help with AI safety” to one bounded thing that matters.

A root safety claim is decomposed into explicit dependencies. Some leaves are machine-checkable, some require formal proof, some need empirical evidence, some need human review, and some remain OPEN. Human-addressable leaves become tasks.

A fair contributor model

Mission-driven does not mean people with scarce skills must work for free.

Volunteer work expands participation. Compensation expands depth, retention, and access for people who cannot donate significant time.

01

Volunteer when you want to

Public-good tasks remain available for people who want to donate an hour or learn by doing.

02

Paid work is visible up front

Funded bounties, paid reviews, and specialist contracts are labeled before anyone commits productive work.

03

L2 opens paid-task eligibility

Verified contributors can enter the general paid marketplace. Payment is tied to the task—not secretly to rank.

04

L4 opens review authority

Review authority is a separate trust decision. It is earned through reliability and review quality, not purchased or gamified.

Public-good economics

Commercial revenue should make the commons stronger.

PCS is designed around a cross-subsidy: grants, donations, sponsorships, and large commercial users can fund the infrastructure and a Safety Bounty Fund. The exact revenue-allocation policy will be set only after legal and financial review, but the direction is explicit.
