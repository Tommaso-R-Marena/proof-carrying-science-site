    RESEARCH PROTOCOL / REALISTIC LIMITS

    What can this actually teach a planning model?

    ProofLab is not collecting “the answer.” It collects repeated decisions among controlled alternatives: which investigation a person chose, which alternatives were feasible, what prerequisite blocked a premature choice, and whether the person needed help. Multiple valid next steps remain valid.

    01

    Prioritize

    Choose the next high-value premise, source fact or cited lemma. Feasible ordering is deterministically checked; usefulness is an empirical question.

    02

    Falsify

    Suggest a collision, dependency, assumption or boundary attack. A proposal is not a counterexample until independently reproduced.

    03

    Calibrate

    Recognize unsupported jumps and uncertain explanations. Learn to abstain rather than claim a theorem was proved by clicking.

    🔒 THE BENCHMARK FIREWALL

    22 training tasks are public. Eight validation and ten evaluation theorem targets remain withheld. All originate in one PCS repository, so cross-project generalization remains untested. Private validation is needed before claiming research progress.
