Research architecture

Distributed contributors should not require distributed trust.

The research goal is to make a large assurance problem decomposable without making the final conclusion depend on the honesty, seniority, or popularity of the people—or AI systems—who propose its parts.

RESEARCH THESIS

Domain-specific meaning should plug into a domain-independent assurance kernel.

PCS separates a typed domain claim, a committed computational world, a proof-obligation graph, local validators, and the rules that allow valid leaves to imply their parents. This is the architecture needed for both cross-domain science and distributed AI-safety work.

AI safety first

Start with claims we can honestly bound.

PCS should not claim to prove that an arbitrary model is globally safe. The first AI-safety program targets properties that can be expressed over exact traces, policies, artifacts, evaluators, and state transitions.

TRACE

Bounded behavior

No declared forbidden action occurs in a committed evaluation trace; cumulative risk remains within an explicit budget.

AUTHORITY

Policy enforcement

Every tool call has appropriate authorization; a consumed authorization cannot be silently reused.

EVALUATION

Evidence integrity

Exact model, evaluator, dataset, policy, configuration, environment, and result commitments remain bound together.

Five research pillars

What the formal program is trying to establish.

01

Domain adapter soundness

A domain can inherit PCS assurance when it supplies local semantics-preservation and validator-soundness obligations rather than assuming the desired conclusion.

02

Obligation-graph soundness

Accepted finite graphs reject cycles, missing dependencies, unsupported leaves, and unresolved obligations; sound local rules compose toward the root.

03

Untrusted proposer non-authority

Human or AI proposal strategies may be arbitrary. Proposal authority alone cannot manufacture assurance if deterministic checks remain authoritative.

04

Executable end-to-end refinement

The actual authority binary and signed package path should refine the same semantics used in the theorem—not a disconnected toy model.

05

External-world transfer is explicit

Proofs over committed bytes do not automatically become claims about a deployed system, a patient, a protein, or the physical world. Data correspondence is a separate premise.

Crowdsourced safety theorem target

Benefit from the crowd without trusting the crowd.

arbitrary contributors+untrusted submissions+verified obligation boundary⇒root assurance only when valid

The key social architecture follows from the same research goal: contributors can propose decompositions, evidence, repairs, attacks, or proofs, but an unverified contribution cannot close a leaf merely because the submitter is respected or because many people agree.

What PCS can establish

Scoped computational assurance.

  • Exact committed artifacts and claims were bound together.
  • Supported checks were replayed under their declared semantics.
  • Accepted graph obligations satisfied their local rules.
  • Formal results hold under their visible hypotheses.
What PCS cannot infer for free

External truth and human intent.

  • That committed bytes faithfully represent an external deployment.
  • That a natural-language statement was what the human “really meant.”
  • That a bounded evaluation proves global AI safety.
  • That empirical adequacy follows from formal consistency.
Cross-domain ambition

AI safety is the wedge. The kernel should remain scientific infrastructure.

AI SAFETY

Agents, policies, evaluations

Trace invariants, authorization, evaluator binding, resource budgets, reproducibility, and red-team evidence.

BIOLOGY

Computational biology

Claims about exact sequences, structures, scores, simulations, and analysis artifacts—with external biological correspondence explicit.

SCIENCE

Other computational disciplines

New adapters can contribute local semantics without modifying the core idea that proofs, replay, evidence, and trust boundaries stay separate.

RESEARCH + PRODUCT

The contributor platform is not separate from PCS. It is a user interface for the obligation graph.

The long-term system turns formally meaningful open obligations into human-scale research work, then routes accepted outputs back into an auditable assurance case.
