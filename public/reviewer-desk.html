Labeling policy

Faithful: the explanation preserves the quantifier, predicates, logical relationship and negations of the submitted FINAL proposition.

Unfaithful: it asserts a different proposition. Ambiguous: it admits multiple interpretations. Unsupported: it makes a real-world or proof guarantee not justified by the bounded game.

Do not infer correctness merely from the mission score. Do not include personal information in rationales. The Founder/Owner retains research export authority.
