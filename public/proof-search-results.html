What this evidence means

Lean checks the formal statements under the recorded assumptions. It does not certify our timing measurements, physical neural execution or the usefulness of a question outside mathematics.

These are small, authored prospective cases, not an independently blind benchmark. Exclusion from four known corpora does not prove conceptual novelty or absence from all prior data. An equal call cap does not equate the internal work of a tactic to one neural proposal. We have no evidence here of accelerating self-improvement or a learned advantage.

The first run recorded setup calls with an outer-call ledger that excludes an internal startup handshake. The repeat records separate compiler, wire and physical setup counts. Search counts agree. Both raw runs are preserved.
